# 06. India GST and Billing Readiness: Software Gap Assessment

**This is a software capability assessment, not legal or tax advice, and it does not assert legal compliance.** Statements about what Indian rules require (for example invoice-number length) are the reviewer's understanding of commonly cited requirements and **must be verified against authoritative sources and the business's accountant** before any real billing. The project itself records that the tax reference data (rates, categories, jurisdiction table) are unreviewed placeholders (R-040, R-083) and that production billing is gated off.

Classification: **Fully supported**, **Partially supported**, **Not supported**, **Unable to determine**. "Fully" means the capability exists and is tested; it does not mean legally certified.

## Summary for an accountant or architect

- **Strong:** seller identity, place-of-supply mechanics, intra/inter-state split, exact arithmetic and rounding policy, immutable snapshots, gap-free numbering.
- **Not usable for real GST invoices yet** because of: the unreviewed tax data, no corrections (credit/debit notes, cancellation), 18-character default invoice numbers, no services, no cess/reverse charge/export/SEZ, no due date and terms, no GST reports, and the production gate.
- **Next-level statutory work** (e-invoice IRN/QR, e-way bill, GSTR-1) is entirely unstarted; the invoice snapshot is a good foundation for it.
