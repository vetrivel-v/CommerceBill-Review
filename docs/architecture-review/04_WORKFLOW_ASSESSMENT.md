## Reading the table

- Workflows 1, 2, 4, 7 to 11 and 20 are real and well tested for **DEMO** billing in the packaged and Electron end-to-end journey.
- Workflows 12 to 14 (corrections) are the largest functional hole: **a mistake in a real invoice cannot be corrected** (R-088, R-094). This is a go-live blocker for real billing.
- Workflows 5, 6, 16, 17, 18, 19 do not exist.
- Workflow 3 works mechanically but its data is deliberately unreviewed.
