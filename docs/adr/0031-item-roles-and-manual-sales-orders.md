# ADR-0031: Item roles, fulfilment mode and manual sales orders (MTO-1)

- Status: Accepted and implemented in MTO-1 (2026-10-29). R-040 stays open; this does not enable production billing.
- Builds on: ADR-0021 (Product is the sellable-item identity), ADR-0022 (stock ledger), ADR-0023 (tax is configuration, never inferred), ADR-0024 (immutable invoices), ADR-0027 (backup and upgrade path)
- Business context: CommerceBill is primarily for MADE-TO-ORDER custom manufacturing (3D printing, laser engraving), not buy-finished-product-and-resell retail. Orders arrive from Amazon, Flipkart, Instagram, phone and by hand; what is bought is raw material, components and packaging.

## Decisions

1. **Product stays the aggregate and the table** (decision 1 of the approved roadmap). The user-facing word is **Items**. `products.kind` (GOODS or SERVICE: the legal/tax nature) is untouched and independent. Nothing about tax is inferred from the new fields.
2. **`item_role`**: SELLABLE, RAW_MATERIAL, COMPONENT, PACKAGING. Every existing row becomes SELLABLE. The role is fixed after creation (trigger), like the kind. Non-sellable items are physical GOODS, have **no** selling price, MRP or price basis (CHECK `products_item_role_shape`), usually track stock, and can be neither ordered nor invoiced (database triggers on `sales_order_lines` and `sales_invoice_lines`, and `ITEM_NOT_SELLABLE` in the invoice engine). The billing item search asks the server for SELLABLE only. A tax category is still stored for every item (it is a reference, as before); making it optional for materials is a later change.
3. **`fulfilment_mode`** for sellable GOODS: STOCKED (finished stock is held; an invoice dispatches it) or MADE_TO_ORDER (no finished stock; an invoice has no stock effect). It is a view of stock tracking, so the database requires STOCKED to track and MADE_TO_ORDER not to, and fills the mode from tracking when a writer does not name it (so an older client, or a restore of an older backup, stays valid). Backfill: tracked goods STOCKED, untracked goods MADE_TO_ORDER, services no mode. The screen keeps the existing "Track inventory" control and shows the resulting fulfilment mode.
4. **Units.** Grams accept one decimal place (`units.decimal_places`); the stock column stays `numeric(18,4)`, so precision is not reduced. SHEET and SPOOL are added for new and existing organizations. There is no unit conversion.
5. **Sales orders** (`sales_orders`, `sales_order_lines`, `sales_order_events`). Orders are numbered `SO/{FY}/...` from the numbering engine (a new SALES_ORDER sequence is provisioned for existing organizations and for new setups). The **buyer, billing and delivery details are snapshots on the order**; the Customer link is optional (`customer_id` nullable) and, when given, only completes empty snapshot fields at the time. Marketplace buyers therefore need no customer master. Invoice recipient validation is not weakened: it will run when an order becomes an invoice (MTO-2).
6. **Channel** (MANUAL, INSTAGRAM, PHONE, AMAZON, FLIPKART, OTHER) is a label only; nothing is called or synchronised. An external order id is unique per organization AND channel when present (partial unique index) and is refused for MANUAL.
7. **Idempotency and concurrency.** Creation carries a client `operationId` (unique per organization) and a request fingerprint: the same request returns the same order, a different one is refused. Edits and status changes carry the loaded `version`; a stale one is refused and nothing is overwritten.
8. **Status model.** Stored: DRAFT, CONFIRMED, NEEDS_INFO, IN_PRODUCTION, READY, CANCELLED, CLOSED. INVOICED and PAID are never stored (they will be derived from invoice links and receivables). Reachable in MTO-1: DRAFT to CONFIRMED or CANCELLED; CONFIRMED to NEEDS_INFO, CANCELLED or CLOSED; NEEDS_INFO to CONFIRMED or CANCELLED. IN_PRODUCTION and READY exist in the vocabulary but **no path leads to them** until production exists (MTO-4). CANCELLED and CLOSED are final. A reason is required to mark an order NEEDS_INFO, to close it, and to cancel one that is not a draft. CONFIRMED needs a delivery address (line and state) and at least one line. The same table of edges is enforced by a database trigger and by the application; a test tries every pair against both. CLOSED means closed by hand (for example finished outside CommerceBill); it creates no invoice and touches no stock.
9. **Lines** hold the item, a snapshot of its SKU, name and unit, the quantity (validated against the unit's decimal places), the **agreed unit price** (typed per order; the item price is only a starting value) and **textual customization**. Totals are exact (BigInt, half-up to the paisa) and are agreed values, not tax. Attachments and design files are **Deferred** until managed storage, metadata, backup inclusion, restore verification and cleanup are designed as one feature (R-102).
10. **History and audit.** `sales_order_events` is append-only (created, edited with field NAMES, status changes with an optional reason). Audit rows (`sales_order.create|update|status|cancel`) carry the order number, channel, statuses and field names, never the buyer, an address or free text; diagnostic logs carry none of it (tested at trace level).
11. **Permissions** reuse `sales.order.read` and `sales.order.write`; no new permission. The organization always comes from the session.
12. **Recovery.** New tables are included in backups automatically. Restore-time integrity checks now verify item roles, order/line/total agreement, that orders reference sellable items of their organization, that invoice lines reference sellable items, and that order numbers were issued by the numbering ledger. The normal Restore still refuses a backup of another schema version (`schema_mismatch`); the supported path for an older installation is the pre-upgrade verified safety backup followed by the migration (tested on a populated pre-MTO database).

## Consequences

- Materials, components and packaging can be recorded and stocked now; purchasing, BOM and production come later (MTO-3, MTO-4).
- Orders cannot yet be invoiced (MTO-2) or produced (MTO-4).
- Orders that were closed by hand can never be reopened; cancel and recreate instead.
